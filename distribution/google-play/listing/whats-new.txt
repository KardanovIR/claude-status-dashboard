- Tap a session to bring its terminal to the front on the Mac running it — the app tells that machine, and the window, tab or pane comes forward.
- If the session has already ended, the card offers Resume: the Mac starts it again where it left off.
- A card no longer shows a false "no answer" when the Mac is just slow — a late reply now corrects it instead of being discarded.
- Machines you own appear on the board with their name, and show when one is offline.
