First release of AgStatus for Android.

- A live board of your coding agent sessions: project, status, and current activity.
- Session timeline: every status change with a timestamp.
- Usage bars for plan limit windows and their reset times.
- Set up by scanning the QR code from your terminal, pasting a board URL, or pairing a machine with a code.
- Demo mode, so you can look around with no server.
- Works with the hosted board or your own self-hosted server.
